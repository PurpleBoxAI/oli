# Kick-off agenda

[date], [place], 45 minutes. [partner] and [owner name]. Bring the pilot brief, the Phase 0 workbook and the self-assessment.

1. Goals in the owner's words (5 min). What would make this worth it by the end? Write it down verbatim; it becomes the success criteria.
2. The pilot in two minutes (3 min). Your accounts, drafts only, you decide everything, the terms, what we ask of you. Confirm agreement to the brief.
3. Self-assessment (7 min). Walk the ten questions together. Note every "no".
4. Business facts (10 min). Services as on the website, ZIP codes served, callback promise, office details, sign-off name. Fill the rules file on the spot.
5. Who owns what (10 min). Ads account, Business Profile, website, the business's Google account, email and calendar in use, current call answering, the operations system and whether it accepts outside leads. Name the franchisor contact for brand approval. Fill workbook section 1.
6. Baseline (5 min). Agree how to count calls received, calls missed, inquiries, ad spend and admin hours for the next two weeks. Set the start date.
7. Next steps and dates (5 min). Franchisor call (who, by when). AI plan check. Call answering choice. Second meeting date.

Leave with: success criteria in the owner's words, rules file mostly filled, workbook section 1 filled, baseline start date, franchisor contact, next meeting date.
