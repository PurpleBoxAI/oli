# Local Growth Pack pilot: [business name]

Prepared by [partner name or "self"] for [owner name]. Draft of [date].

## What this is

A pilot of about twelve weeks (estimate, not a commitment) of a simple way to run three things that take time you do not have: answering and logging every inquiry call, following up every lead on time, and seeing once a month what your marketing did. An AI assistant does the reading and drafting. You stay the only person who talks to customers and spends money.

Everything runs in your own accounts: your email, your calendar, a sheet in your Drive, your AI subscription. Nobody else hosts anything or keeps your customer data.

## What you get

- Every missed call answered by a call answering service running an intake script you approve, with each inquiry written to one sheet (the Lead Log).
- Every business day, a short list of who still needs a callback, a draft confirmation email for each, and a reminder on your calendar. You review, send, call.
- Every week, draft replies to new reviews and one draft post for your Business Profile, ready to paste.
- Every week, a review of your ads with at most three recommended changes and the exact place to make them. You decide.
- Every month, one page: leads by source, what they cost, how fast you called back, three things that worked, three to fix.
- At the end, everything stays with you and keeps running. Nothing to cancel, nothing to migrate.

## What we ask of you

- Business facts at kick-off: services as on your website, service ZIP codes, the callback promise you can keep, office details.
- Approvals: the intake script, the rules the assistant follows, every draft before it goes out. Brand approval from your franchisor where required.
- Two weeks of baseline numbers before anything changes: calls received, calls missed, inquiries, ad spend, hours spent on admin.
- About two hours a week once running (a target we measure, not a promise): ten minutes most mornings, twenty on Mondays, ten on Thursdays, thirty on the first of the month.
- Five test calls before go-live.
- A short weekly check-in for the first six weeks, if a partner is delivering.
- [If applicable] Permission to write a short case study with the results, which you approve before use.

## What it costs

[Pilot terms. For example: "The pilot is free. You pay only for what you already own or choose to buy: your AI subscription, a call answering service, and your ad spend."]

## What it is not

Not a CRM, not an ad agency, not an autopilot. The assistant never sends an email, never changes an ad, never quotes a price or a staff name, and never collects more about a customer than the one field your business needs.

## Timeline (example)

- Week 1: kick-off, facts, access check, franchisor call, baseline starts.
- Weeks 1 to 2: Business Profile clean-up, Lead Log, intake script, call answering, assistant setup, test calls.
- Weeks 2 to 4: daily lead follow-up live.
- Weeks 3 to 6: review replies, posts and weekly ads review live.
- Weeks 6 to 8: first monthly report, first script tuning.
- Weeks 8 to 12: it runs; we measure against the baseline.
