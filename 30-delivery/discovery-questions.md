# Discovery questions

Ask before anything is built. Record answers in the Phase 0 workbook. Blank is better than a guess.

## For the owner

Business
1. Which services do you offer, exactly as they appear on your website?
2. Which ZIP codes do you serve? Any you do not serve even if asked?
3. What callback time can you promise and keep, on a normal day and on a bad day?
4. Who answers the phone today, and what happens to a call nobody picks up?
5. Roughly how many inquiry calls a week? How many are job seekers?
6. Where do inquiries get written down today, if anywhere?

Marketing
7. Are you running ads? Which channels? Who set them up and who can log in?
8. Who manages your Business Profile? When was it last updated?
9. How do new reviews get answered today?
10. What has been tried before and why did it stop?

Time and tools
11. How many hours a week do you spend on admin and marketing today?
12. Which email and calendar do you use for the business? Which AI subscription, if any?
13. Which operations or scheduling system does the business run on? Does it accept leads from outside tools?
14. What is the one thing you most want to stop doing yourself?

Approvals and limits
15. Is there anything the assistant must never say or do with customers beyond the standard rules?
16. What is the one personal-data field your intake needs (for child care: number of children and age ranges)? Anything else we should never collect?

## For the franchisor (if a franchise)

1. Who owns and administers the ads account for this location? May the owner have read access?
2. Who owns the Business Profile and the location page on the website?
3. Are outside vendors or tools allowed for call answering and marketing? Is there an approved list?
4. What is the brand approval process for an intake script, a Business Profile post and a review reply? Turnaround time?
5. Does your operations system accept leads from outside tools, or does the owner enter them by hand?
6. Is there a brand guide the assistant's rules should follow? May we have a copy?
7. Is corporate running any ads that overlap with this territory?
8. Would you want the same package offered to other locations if the pilot works?
