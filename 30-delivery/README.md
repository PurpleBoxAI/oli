# Delivery kit

Material for starting the method with a business, whether you are the owner doing it yourself or a partner delivering it. Everything is brand-neutral; fill the [brackets] for your business and keep the filled copies in your own files.

- Pilot brief template: what the pilot is, what the owner gets, what the owner commits to, what it costs.
- Kick-off agenda: a 45-minute first meeting that ends with success criteria, business facts and a baseline start date.
- Discovery questions: what to ask the owner and the franchisor before anything is built.
- Phase 0 workbook: access inventory, franchisor questions, two-week baseline tally, eligibility check, go or no-go.

Use them in that order. The phase plan they feed is in the method, phase plan.
