# Local Growth Pack

Status: draft, not yet released or tested with a real business.

A free, open method for a small local service business (a franchise location, an independent office) to run Google visibility, lead follow-up and monthly reporting with the AI subscription it already pays for.

Everything runs in your own accounts: your email, your Google account, your sheet, your Claude or ChatGPT subscription. Nothing is hosted by anyone else and no customer data leaves your systems.

## Start here

1. [The method](/method/): what to do, with no technology named. Read the overview and the self-assessment first.
2. [Implementation](/implementation/): how to run it on Claude or on ChatGPT, in your own accounts, with a verification checklist.
3. [Shared templates](/implementation/shared/): the rules file, the intake call script and the Lead Log.
4. [Delivery kit](/delivery/): pilot brief, kick-off agenda, discovery questions and the Phase 0 workbook, for starting with a real business.

## Guardrails every part of the package follows

- Drafts only. The assistant never sends an email or message; you do.
- Ads are read-only. The assistant recommends; you make the change.
- Minimum personal data. Nothing the workflow does not need.
- No invented numbers. Missing data is reported as missing, with the source named.

## Ways to use it

Do it yourself with the guides here. Or have an implementation partner set it up in your accounts and hand over, or set it up and keep running the monthly review for you. The method is the same in all three cases.
