# 1. Overview and outcomes

## Who this is for

An owner of a local service business with one to a few territories and no marketing staff. A franchise location or an independent office. Typical examples: in-home child care, senior care, cleaning, pet care, home repair. The owner often covers the front desk, so marketing gets whatever time is left, which is usually none.

## The problem, in the owner's words

- The business does not show up, or does not win the click, in Google Search and Maps.
- Inquiry calls go unanswered or are not written down, and there is no consistent way to take an inquiry.
- Nobody follows up on leads on time, so good leads go cold.
- There is no monthly picture of what the marketing money did.

## What "working" looks like

- Every inquiry call is answered, every inquiry is written down in one place, and the owner knows which ones still need a callback.
- Every lead gets a follow-up within the time the business promised.
- Google reviews get a reply, and the business profile gets a fresh post every week.
- The ads get one review a week, and the owner makes at most a few small changes based on real numbers.
- Once a month the owner sees one page: where the leads came from, what they cost, how fast the business called back, what to fix next.
- The owner spends a small, predictable amount of time on all of this. The design target is under two hours a week (a target to validate, not a measured figure).

## Principles

1. Your systems, your data. The work runs in the owner's own email, calendar, spreadsheet, Google accounts and AI subscription. No outside party hosts anything or holds customer data.
2. The assistant drafts; the owner decides. No email is sent, no ad is changed and no script is updated without the owner doing it.
3. Minimum personal data. The business collects only what it needs to call someone back and serve them.
4. No invented numbers. Every figure in a report comes from a named record. Missing data is reported as missing.
5. Small changes, measured. At most a few recommended changes per week, each with a reason, each checked the following week.
6. Brand rules win. For a franchise location, the franchisor's brand guide decides wording, and the franchisor approves customer-facing language.

## What this is not

It is not a CRM, an ad agency or an autopilot. It is a way of working, with templates and an assistant that does the reading and drafting. The owner stays in charge of every customer contact and every dollar.

## Ways to use this package

- Do it yourself, with the guides in `20-implementation/`.
- Have an implementation partner set it up in your accounts and hand it over.
- Have a partner set it up and keep running the monitoring and improvement phase for you.

The method is the same in all three cases.
