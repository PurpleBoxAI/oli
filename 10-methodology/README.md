# Level 1: The method

This folder describes how a small local service business runs Google visibility, lead follow-up and monthly reporting with the help of an AI assistant. It names no product. Everything here works with any capable AI assistant that can read a spreadsheet, draft an email and set a reminder. The stack-specific setup lives in `20-implementation/`.

Read in this order:

1. `01-overview-and-outcomes.md`: the problem, what "working" looks like, the principles.
2. `02-operating-model.md`: the five recurring workflows and their cadence.
3. `03-roles.md`: who does what, who approves what.
4. `04-data-model.md`: the Lead Log, its fields, statuses and timestamps.
5. `05-guardrails.md`: the hard rules every workflow follows.
6. `06-metrics.md`: what is measured, how, and from which record.
7. `07-phase-plan.md`: from "get ready" to "monitor and improve".
8. `08-intake-process.md`: how an inquiry call is handled, as a process.
9. `09-self-assessment.md`: a one-page readiness check.

Status: draft, not yet tested with a real business. Numbers in these documents are design defaults or examples unless a source is named.
