# 9. Self-assessment

Answer yes or no. Count the yes answers at the end.

## Readiness

1. I can name my services exactly as they appear on my website.
2. I can list the ZIP codes I serve.
3. I know who owns my Google Ads account, my Business Profile and my website (me, or my franchisor).
4. I can promise a callback time (for example, same business day) and keep it.
5. I have, or can set up, a paid AI assistant subscription that connects to my email, calendar and spreadsheet.
6. I have, or can set up, a way to answer calls I miss and write them to a sheet.
7. I can spend about two hours a week on this, in short daily and weekly slots.
8. I am willing to send every customer email myself and make every ad change myself.
9. If I am a franchise location, I know who approves brand wording and how long that takes.
10. I can record two weeks of baseline numbers before changing anything.

## Reading the result

- 9 or 10 yes: ready to start Phase 0 on your own or with a partner.
- 6 to 8 yes: start Phase 0, but resolve the "no" answers before Phase 1. The most common blocker is account ownership (question 3).
- 5 or fewer: the method will not hold yet. Fix the basics first: a service list, a callback promise, and knowing who controls your Google accounts.

## Which path fits

- Do it yourself: you answered yes to 5, 6 and 7, and you are comfortable connecting tools to your accounts.
- Implementation package: you want the setup done in your accounts and handed over with a checklist.
- Implementation plus management: you want someone to run the monthly review and improvement phase and report to you.

In every path the work stays in your accounts and you keep every approval.
