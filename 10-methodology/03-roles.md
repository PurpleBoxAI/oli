# 3. Roles

## Owner

- Supplies the business facts: services, territory, ZIP codes, callback promise, office details.
- Approves the intake script, the rules file and every customer-facing draft.
- Sends every email, makes every call, makes every ad change, updates the Lead Log status.
- Reads the monthly report and picks next month's fixes.

## Office staff (if any)

- Can run Workflow A and update the Lead Log on the owner's behalf.
- Does not change ads or scripts.

## AI assistant

- Reads the Lead Log, the ad numbers and the reviews.
- Drafts emails, replies and posts. Creates reminders. Writes the monthly report.
- Recommends changes. Never sends, never changes an ad, never changes the intake script.
- Reports missing data as missing.

## Call answering (AI receptionist or answering service)

- Answers calls the office does not pick up.
- Runs the intake script, collects the minimum fields, routes job seekers to the careers page, promises a callback.
- Writes each inquiry to the Lead Log.
- Never quotes prices, availability or staff names.

## Franchisor (franchise locations only)

- Approves brand wording in the intake script, posts and review replies.
- Confirms who owns the Google Ads account, the Business Profile and the website, and which outside vendors are allowed.
- May adopt the package for all locations.

## Implementation partner (optional)

- Sets up the templates, the connections and the scheduled runs in the owner's accounts.
- Hands over with a verification checklist.
- If retained, runs the monitoring and improvement phase and reports to the owner.
- Never holds the owner's credentials or customer data after hand-over.

## Approval map

- Customer-facing email: owner.
- Review reply: owner (brand check for a franchise).
- Business Profile post: owner, plus franchisor brand approval for a franchise.
- Ad change: owner, in the ad account.
- Intake script change: owner, plus franchisor brand approval for a franchise.
- Rules file change: owner.
